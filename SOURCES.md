**Dimensions.** A standard ramen donburi is about 21–24 cm across and 8–10 cm deep. Modelled: 23 cm rim, 9.3 cm tall, foot ring 8.5 cm, wall 4.5 mm, broth 7 cm deep. Noodle Ø ≈ 1.9 mm (medium noodles are about 1.2–2 mm). Half egg 5.5 × 4 cm. Chashu slices about 7 × 6 cm, 4–6 mm thick.

**Facts behind the one-liners** (common culinary knowledge, cross-checked against Wikipedia "Ramen", "Kansui", "Chashu", "Menma", "Ajitsuke tamago", "Auricularia" and "Enokitake"):
- Tare is the concentrated seasoning that goes in the bowl first, and its type (shoyu, shio, miso) names the ramen.
- Aromatic oil (koumi-yu) is a flavoured fat such as chicken fat, lard or garlic oil, spooned on top.
- Ramen noodles are wheat noodles made with kansui, alkaline mineral water, which makes them springy and yellowish.
- Ramen chashu is pork belly, rolled, tied and braised in soy-based liquid.
- Ajitama is a soft-boiled egg marinated in soy-based liquid.
- Menma is lacto-fermented bamboo shoot, dried and then simmered in seasoning.
- Wood ear (kikurage) grows on wood, has little taste and a crunchy, gelatinous texture.
- The red square-spiral border on ramen bowls is the "thunder" pattern (raimon), from Chinese bronze decoration.
